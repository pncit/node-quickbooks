## design-auditor — round 1

Reviewed the design against the actual fork at `HEAD` and against the live `npm audit` advisory
state. The core strategy (re-sync onto upstream's axios codebase, re-apply `refreshTokenCallBack` in
both `index.js` and `index.d.ts`, preserve the error-isolation contract) is sound and well-reasoned,
and the feature regions are correctly located. However, one load-bearing security claim — that
bumping `fast-xml-parser` to `^4.5.6` clears its issue-#3 exposure — is contradicted by the current
advisory database, which puts the whole 4.x line inside the vulnerable range. That defeats the
"clean `npm audit`" success criterion the entire design exists to satisfy. A second issue is a
current-state narrative that misorders the fork's divergence relative to the 2.0.46 sync.
